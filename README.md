Business Problem Summary
Wanjiku operates a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and has recorded two weeks of daily sales and rough stock counts in a notebook. Facing critical business questions regarding overall growth, inventory levels, and operational focus, she hired a freelance data analyst to evaluate her raw records using core Python. The goal is to uncover revenue trends, identify stockouts risks, and provide actionable insights.

Key Findings & Report Highlights
Total 14-Day Sales: KES 46,450.00
Average Daily Sales:KES 3,317.86
Week-on-Week Growth: 10.66% increase from Week 1 to Week 2.
Weekend Premium: Weekend sales averaged KES 4,900.00, representing an 82.5% surge compared to weekdays.
Restock Alerts: Tomatoes and Sukuma are currently at or below reorder levels and require immediate restocking.

Business Recommendations
Wanjiku should immediately restock Tomatoes and Sukuma to avoid stockouts during peak periods. Given that weekend revenue surges by **82.5%**, inventory preparation should be intensified on Thursdays and Fridays. Finally, with overall sales growing by **10.66%**, she can confidently expand stock on high-margin items like **Avocados** (gross margin **42.9%**) to maximize profits
